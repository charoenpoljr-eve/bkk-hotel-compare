Hotel shortlist from Rose

Hotels near Asok, Phrom Phong and Nana BTS. 4★ and up, all with a fitness centre. Tap a station to filter, and tap “Compare” on up to 4 hotels to see them side by side.

Want to compare? Tap + Add to compare on 2–4 hotels, then press Compare now in the bar at the bottom.

Prices are estimates for a standard room per night around 2 December (high season, about 10% above usual), not live rates. Check Agoda or Booking for your dates. Star class and amenities come from general hotel info and Google Maps, so confirm with the hotel. Walk times are rough. All hotels have a fitness centre.

Airport times are rough car estimates; 60–90 minutes or more at rush hour. The Airport Rail Link to Makkasan (25–30 min), then MRT to Asok, avoids traffic.

“Guest score” hotels are newer additions. Their rating is a rough 5-point conversion of review-site scores, not Google.

Price notes

  • Tax: if a price is shown as “++”, add 10% service charge + 7% VAT (about +17.7%). Agoda and Booking usually include it. Example: 3,000 → about 3,530.
  • Higher: Christmas and New Year, weekends, better rooms, breakfast, flexible cancellation. Lower: pre-paid rates, longer stays, member deals.

Sights, shops and rooftops, grouped by area. For places across the city, the three boxes show how many train stops to ride from each station (BTS or MRT, counting the change to the MRT at Asok).
